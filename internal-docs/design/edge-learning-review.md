# Edge decision learning

Edge explains historical price consequences, with automatic evidence context.
It does not grade skill, approve a trading policy, or infer trade intent.

## Authority and scope

The pure Edge engine owns coverage by action and direction, monthly samples,
concentration, and comparisons of the same decisions across horizons. The daemon
owns selection and exact linkage to retained local protection records. CLI, MCP,
and SPA render the typed result. Existing materiality gates remain reporting
filters, never trading-policy thresholds.

A useful observation identifies its action, direction, horizon, scored/eligible
count, and evidence limitations. It cannot become a strength/drag or instruction
to change position size. Monthly totals and concentration are descriptive, not
confidence estimates or proof of independent opportunities.

Matched horizon comparisons include only changes scored at both endpoints.
They disclose the common sample and paired differences; the existing all-sample
matrix remains a separate descriptive view. Execution notional uses execution
FX for a common coverage denominator; unknown amounts remain unknown and known
subtotals cannot be labeled complete coverage.

Option position cycles require exact-contract raw execution chronology and a
reconciled quantity path from flat to flat. Cross-contract strategy identity is
not inferred. Incomplete openings, events, transfers, contradictory markers and
ambiguous fills remain explicit exclusions. A selected-window cycle result
never replaces the separate broker realized and dated open-P/L scopes.

Risk context requires exact broker execution identity joined to a uniquely
consistent scoped local submitted protection proposal. It describes local
protection provenance, not broker-confirmed intent or proven risk effectiveness.
Missing, conflicting and partial links cannot assign a purpose to a whole change.
Publication binds local context separately from broker evidence. Read-time checks
withhold links when that basis changes. Optional order and proposal reads each
stop at 10,000 records; reaching the cap withholds context rather than trusting
a prefix. Proposal records remain in the daemon scope, with account/mode checked
after retrieval. This conservative cap can withhold otherwise valid context on
a large multi-account history; it cannot change financial results.

## Acceptance

- Hand-calculated matched samples, exclusion/amount coverage and concentration.
- Correlated market entries and risk-reducing exits cannot become skill claims.
- Missing and conflicting context never changes price-impact arithmetic.
- Option adds/partial closes/reopening, short cycles, missing openings,
  ambiguous chronology, lifecycle events and anchor mismatch.
- Deterministic results under reordered evidence and persisted reload.
- CLI, MCP, HTTP and SPA preserve the same typed evidence and unavailable states.
- Rendered desktop and narrow viewport QA; no broker writes or pairing changes.
